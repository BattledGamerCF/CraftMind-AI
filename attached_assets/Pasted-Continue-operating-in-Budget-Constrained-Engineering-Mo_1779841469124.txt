Behavioral Continuity and Habit Formation.

Primary objective:
make the bot feel increasingly consistent and familiar over long multiplayer sessions through lightweight learned preferences and repeated behavioral patterns.

Do NOT implement:
- emotions

This phase is purely:
- behavioral adaptation

1. Habit Formation System

Implement lightweight habit tracking.

The bot should gradually learn:
- preferred idle areas
- commonly used storage locations
- favorite routes
- commonly visited zones
- frequently followed players
- preferred crafting/workshop locations

Store only compact weighted counters.

Examples:
- “usually stands near this corner of home”
- “often deposits items in this chest”
- “typically uses this route to the mine”

Habits should influence probability weights, not hardcode behavior.

2. Familiarity Scores

Add lightweight familiarity scoring for:
- locations
- routes
- players
- zones

Familiarity should subtly reduce:
- hesitation
- scanning frequency
- random look behavior
- route correction frequency

Known places should feel calmer and more confident.

3. Routine Behavior

Allow small routine patterns to emerge naturally.

Examples:
- returning to a familiar resting spot when idle
- preferring a known chest
- using the same workshop area repeatedly
- standing in familiar safe positions near players

Keep routines subtle and interruptible.

Do NOT make the bot rigid or repetitive.

4. Social Preference Weighting

The bot should gradually bias toward:
- frequently interacted-with players
- trusted players
- recently cooperative players

Examples:
- slightly prefer staying near trusted players
- respond faster to familiar players
- prioritize known players during group follow situations

Keep this lightweight and behavioral only.

5. Contextual Memory Influence

Recent events should temporarily influence behavior.

Examples:
recent explosion near home:
- avoid area briefly
- higher alertness nearby

recent successful mining trip:
- slightly higher exploration confidence

recent repeated path failures:
- reduce preference for that route temporarily

Use decaying temporary weights.

6. Ambient Location Preference

In safe/home zones:
- allow the bot to develop preferred idle spots over time

Requirements:
- never block players
- never block workstations
- never block doors/chests
- remain interruptible

The goal is:
subtle familiarity, not scripted staging.

7. Movement Confidence

Improve movement confidence in familiar areas:
- fewer hesitation pauses
- smoother turning
- fewer random scans
- reduced “uncertain” movement

Dangerous or unfamiliar areas should still feel cautious.

8. Adaptive Exploration Bias

Exploration should gradually:
- avoid historically dangerous regions
- prefer previously successful areas
- bias toward resource-rich routes
- avoid repeatedly unproductive wandering

No complex map systems.
Use lightweight weighted heuristics only.

9. Anti-Repetition Safeguards

Avoid over-learning.

Add:
- decay over time
- randomness floor
- variety weighting
- route diversification

The bot should feel familiar, not robotic.

10. Cost Discipline

Continue minimizing:
- broad scans
- repeated typechecks
- unnecessary reasoning passes
- architecture churn
- speculative rewrites

Use:
- lightweight counters
- weighted heuristics
- compact memory structures
- local patches only

The current priority is:
long-session continuity, familiarity, and subtle emergent behavior.
