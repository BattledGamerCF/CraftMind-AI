Environmental Awareness and Memory-Driven Presence.

Primary objective:
make the bot feel increasingly aware of the world and player-created spaces without becoming intrusive, noisy, or computationally expensive.

Do NOT increase randomness significantly.

Focus instead on:
- contextual behavior
- memory continuity
- environmental understanding
- subtle situational awareness

1. Environmental Zones

Implement lightweight semantic environmental zones around important places.

Examples:
- home
- storage area
- workshop
- mine entrance
- farm
- dangerous area
- social/common area

Zones should be inferred heuristically from:
- chest density
- crafting blocks
- beds
- torches
- repeated visits
- waypoint clustering

Store only compact summaries.

Behavior should adapt slightly by zone:
home:
- relaxed
- lower movement frequency
- more idle behaviors

danger:
- higher alertness
- reduced ambient actions
- tighter movement

storage/workshop:
- avoid blocking containers/workstations
- less wandering

Do NOT build a giant spatial simulation.

2. Memory-Driven Familiarity

The bot should gradually behave differently in familiar places.

Examples:
- smoother movement near home
- reduced scanning in known-safe areas
- more confidence near familiar routes
- less hesitation around known storage/work areas

Keep this subtle.

No fake emotions or roleplay systems.

3. Contextual Attention

Improve environmental attention logic.

The bot should occasionally notice:
- newly placed blocks nearby
- explosions
- fires
- opened chests
- nearby combat
- unusual movement

Reactions should be lightweight:
- brief look
- reposition
- short acknowledgement
- temporary alertness increase

Avoid spam reactions.

4. Spatial Etiquette

Improve movement etiquette around player builds.

Avoid:
- standing on crops
- standing in doorways
- blocking staircases
- pacing on beds
- standing directly on crafting tables/chests

Add lightweight avoidance heuristics for “important blocks.”

5. Adaptive Ambient Behavior

Ambient actions should adapt by:
- operational state
- playstyle
- nearby players
- environmental zone
- recent activity level

Example:
A relaxed companion bot at home:
- slightly more ambient behavior

A focused worker in a mine:
- almost none

6. Familiar Route Memory

Add lightweight route familiarity.

Track:
- commonly traveled paths
- repeated navigation routes
- recently successful routes

Use this only to:
- reduce hesitation
- reduce unnecessary rerouting
- improve movement confidence

Do NOT implement full navigation graph systems.

7. Event Memory

Store compact short-term notable events:
- recent combat
- nearby death
- explosions
- failed tasks
- successful builds
- player interactions

Use event recency to influence behavior subtly.

Example:
recent creeper explosion nearby:
- increased alertness
- lower ambient idle behavior
- safer movement preference for a short time

8. Presence Without Noise

The bot should increasingly feel “present” while speaking less.

Prioritize:
- positioning
- movement
- timing
- awareness
- subtle reactions

over:
- frequent chat
- repetitive acknowledgements
- constant commentary

Silence is often more believable than chatter.

9. Stability Requirements

All awareness systems must:
- remain lightweight
- avoid excessive scanning
- reuse Perception data
- avoid planner churn
- avoid duplicate reactions
- respect Arbitrator priorities

Do NOT add expensive environmental analysis loops.

10. Cost Discipline

Continue minimizing:
- broad scans
